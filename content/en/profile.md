Tomasz builds scalable business applications as a Junior Low-Code Developer at Consdata, working on the Eximee platform with BPMN process design, REST integrations and WCAG-compliant UI.

Before that, he spent three years at EPAM Systems remediating accessibility issues and standardizing design tokens across large-scale projects, and two years at Acturis Limited building dynamic, data-driven document generation for insurance software.

Recent work at Consdata includes migrating 12 banking processes from a Java-based ServiceMix integration to BPMS processes with soap-proxy, and building an AI agent with Claude Code that aligns a bank's UI artifacts with a new Figma design.

Outside his day job, Tomasz designs and ships complete personal products — Lexicon, Sprout and WhereItWent — by directing AI coding agents through the entire lifecycle: requirements, architecture decisions, implementation, bug fixes and deployment. Each one is a real, installable offline-first PWA used daily, not a tutorial project. This portfolio site is itself built the same way.

His goal is to move professionally into AI and grow there: using it to develop software and to automate processes. He sees low-code as part of that path, since low-code platforms are one way companies bring AI into their business.

He's looking for a role that uses new technologies and where he can fulfil himself, one where low-code platform experience, accessibility rigor and fluency in directing AI-assisted development turn into shipped product, not just prototypes.
